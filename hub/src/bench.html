PREPARE IN THIS ORDER

  1. Power off and isolate one module. Disconnect USB and external power. Remove the LED strip, LED adapter, other sensors and capacitors from this test. Use the HW-870’s printed VCC / GND / DO / AO labels, never wire colors or assumed pin order.
  2. Connect power wires while off. HW-870 VCC → UNO 5V, GND → UNO GND. Leave DO and AO disconnected. This is the UNO’s 5V header, not VIN or a separate LED supply.
  3. Prepare the separate sketch first. Save the current sketch. Open this download in its own HW870_Sensor_Bench_v1 sketch folder and verify for the actual UNO R4 WiFi. Only if Gary chooses to replace the running program, select the correct board/port and upload this diagnostic. Its setup makes D2 and A0 inputs. Keep DO/AO disconnected during that preparation.
  4. Power off again before adding signals. With the diagnostic ready, disconnect USB/power. Wire DO → D2 and AO → A0. Recheck the printed labels, then reconnect USB for this sensor-only setup. Keep the external LED adapter disconnected.
  5. Open Serial Monitor at 115200 baud. Look for HW870_SENSOR_BENCH_V1. An identifying header repeats every 5 seconds; samples are scheduled every 100 ms. Ignore readings from unconnected inputs.
  6. Compare away versus close. Try a small light-colored card or paper close to the sensing face, without touching pins. Record raw DO and AO with the target away and near. If needed, adjust the blue threshold pot a little and compare again. Learn which way this module’s outputs change; do not assume active-low or a particular AO direction.

WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN

Raw signals, no guesses.

ms,DO_raw,AO_raw_0_to_1023: elapsed milliseconds, D2’s raw 0/1 and A0’s relative 10-bit ADC count. The sketch explicitly calls analogReadResolution(10). These counts are not millimetres or a calibrated distance.

Record target, spacing, room lighting, pot setting and both readings. A useful demo today is a repeatable raw change, not an assumed staircase-range detection.

CLOSE RANGE, NOT ToF

Start millimetres away.

These are TCRT5000-family reflectance modules. Vishay specifies a 2.5 mm peak reflectance distance for its sensor under test conditions; that is not a guaranteed range for this module.

They are not time-of-flight sensors or cross-stair rangers. No reflective tape is required for this simple comparison, and tape would not turn them into metre-range sensors.

LEAVE THE LED SYSTEM OUT TODAY

The full reel exceeds this adapter’s rating.

The WS2812E bag says 5V / 5m / 90W: 18A at the labeled full-reel rating. The YS-0510DC adapter says 5V / 10A / 50W. It is below that full-reel rating. Do not route LED current through the UNO or a breadboard.

The strip’s input arrow and connector polarity remain unverified. The 1000µF / 16V capacitor kit and visible loose 1000µF / 10V capacitor stay disconnected until wiring and polarity are verified.
